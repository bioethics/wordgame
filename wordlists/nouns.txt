# Nouns — The Binder's list, and The Sculptor's. Two of these stacked together
# count as a word, so DOOM and HAT make DOOMHAT; a word on the list (or the
# plural of one) gets The Sculptor's ×2. One entry per line; add or strike
# freely, nothing else needs changing.
#
# Singulars only, on purpose. The Sculptor reads a plural back to its singular
# and looks that up (readsAsNoun in js/patrons.js), so CATS pays without CATS
# being here — while The Binder, who needs two entries to fit one rack, keeps
# stacking the short singular forms alone.
#
# Built from the lemmatised word list in "Unused Word lists": a lemma whose
# only inflection is a plural is a noun, which catches 5788 of them. Nouns
# that are also verbs (HOUSE, FIRE, WATER) and those with irregular plurals
# (MAN, MOUSE, TOOTH) slip through that test, so they were added by hand —
# they are the short concrete words that compound best.
#
# Only entries of 3-8 letters are here: two nouns must fit the rack together,
# so nothing longer could ever be set. An entry the dictionary lacks is
# harmless, it simply never comes up.
